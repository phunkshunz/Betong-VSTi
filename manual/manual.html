01Welcome

BETONG is a drum machine and sampler for hard, hypnotic techno. You load sounds onto 16 pads, play them from an 8-track step sequencer, and push everything through a filter, a dub delay and a rumble that ducks under the kick.

The idea is simple: give every track its own length and its own rate. A 16-step hat against a 13-step perc against a 5-tuplet clap. They drift apart and meet again, so the groove keeps moving without you touching anything.

  • 16 pads and 215 original 24-bit sounds, built around a core of clean, fat drum-machine drums, free to use in your own tracks
  • 8-track sequencer with accents and ghost notes, pitch per step, ratchets, probability and swing per track
  • Four scenes, a fill button, and SVAJ and GLITCH to make the machine sway and break up
  • Euclidean magic: patterns that rotate and breathe by themselves, accent, ratchet and pitch layers, tracks that fill each other's gaps, and a ring view
  • A techno filter on every track with drive, envelope and bit crush
  • Dub delay, rumble, master sweep, clipper and stutter
  • 38 kits, 169 patterns and 54 effect presets in genre folders, plus dice that invent new ones

02Install

After buying you get one zip file for Mac and one for Windows. Use the one for your computer.

Mac

macOS 11 Big Sur or later. Intel and Apple Silicon.

  1. Double-click the zip file. You get a folder called BETONG.
  2. Open Terminal and type bash and a space.
  3. Drag the file installera.command from the folder into the Terminal window and press Enter.
  4. Restart your DAW, or rescan plugins. Search for BETONG.

The script copies the VST3 and AU to your user plugin folders and clears the macOS download block. The folder also has BETONG.app, which runs without a DAW.

Windows

64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

  1. Right-click the zip file and choose Extract all. You get a folder called BETONG.
  2. Double-click installera.bat. Click Yes when Windows asks for administrator rights.
  3. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway.
  4. Restart your DAW, or rescan plugins. Search for BETONG.

The plugin goes to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. The folder also has BETONG.exe, which runs without a DAW.

Updates

New versions are free. Download the new zip and run the install script again. It replaces the old version.

03Your first beat in one minute

  1. Put BETONG on an instrument track in your DAW and open it.
  2. Press play in the DAW. BETONG follows the DAW tempo and starts playing right away. In the standalone app, press SPELA (play) at the bottom right.
  3. Pick a kit in the first menu at the top, and a pattern in the second. The menus are sorted in genre folders. Start with the kits under Rena grundkit (clean basic kits).
  4. Press the ALLT (everything) dice. You get a new kit, pattern and effects in one go.
  5. Turn GALENSKAP (madness) up and press ALLT again. It gets weirder. Didn't like it? Press Ångra (undo).
  6. Hold STUTTER on a build-up, and turn SVEP (sweep) left to filter the whole mix.
Good to know

Everything in BETONG is saved with your DAW project: sounds, pads, all four scenes, the Euclidean settings and every knob. The window can be resized from the bottom right corner.

The dice

ButtonWhat it does
KITNew sounds on all pads that are not locked. Kicks stay on pads 1–2, hats on 3–4, claps on 5–6, and so on, so the result still makes sense.
FXNudges the delay, rumble and filters in new directions.

GALENSKAP (madness) sets how wild the dice are. Low: straight four-on-the-floor, normal lengths, sounds in their usual place. High: odd lengths like 5, 7 and 13, tuplets, ratchets, probability, pitched and reversed sounds, and strange sounds on unexpected pads.

Lås (lock) on a sequencer row protects that track and its pad from the dice. Lock your kick and bass, and roll the rest.

Swing and tempo display

SWING pushes every second step later. It works on 1/16, 1/8, 1/16T and 1/32 tracks. The 5- and 7-tuplet tracks are never swung, which makes them sound even more off against the rest.

At the top right you see the tempo. It says BPM · VÄRD (host) when the DAW is playing, and BPM when BETONG uses its own tempo.

06Sounds, pads and chopping

Loading a sound

There are five ways to get a sound onto a pad:

  • Right-click a pad and pick a built-in sound from the categories, or choose Ladda fil... (load file).
  • Drag a file from Finder or Explorer onto a pad, or onto the waveform to load it to the selected pad.
  • Drag several files at once onto a pad. They fill that pad and the ones after it, in order.
  • Press Ladda ljud (load sound) above the waveform to pick a file for the selected pad.
  • Press Bibliotek (library) and click a sound in the list.

File types: WAV, AIFF, FLAC and OGG. MP3 also works on Mac.

The library

The library opens over the waveform and pads. Pick a category on the left, then click a sound on the right. It loads straight onto the selected pad and plays, so you can click through sounds while the beat runs. Press Stäng (close) when you are done.

The built-in categories are kicks, hats and cymbals, clap and snare, perc, bass, stabs and chords, leads, textures and drones, voices, FX, and loops at 130 BPM. All tonal sounds are in F minor, so they fit together.

Your own sounds. Press + Lägg till mapp (add folder) and pick a folder with samples. Its sounds show up under Egna mappar (my folders). BETONG remembers your folders in every project. Ta bort mapp (remove folder) removes the chosen folder from the list. It does not delete any files.

The pads

Click a pad to play it and select it. Each pad shows its sound name, its number, and which track plays it, for example PAD 5 · SPÅR 3. The coloured bar on top matches the track colour. Pads flash when they play.

  • Hi-hat choke: pad 3 (closed hat) cuts off pad 4 (open hat), just like on a real drum machine.
  • One voice per track: a new hit on a track cuts off the last one. This keeps basses and acid lines tight.

Shaping a sound

The waveform shows the sound on the selected pad. The bright part is what the pad plays.

  • Drag the orange edges to move the start and end.
  • Click inside the waveform to hear the pad.
  • Thin lines with numbers show other pads that use the same sound, for example after chopping.

Under the filter on the right there are five controls for the selected pad: PITCH (±24 semitones), DECAY (shortens the sound), PAD VOLYM (−30 to +6 dB), PAD PAN and Baklänges (reverse). Use pad volume to balance a kit.

Chopping a sound onto the pads

Chopping cuts one sound into pieces and puts them on pads 1–16. It is the fastest way to turn a loop or a vocal into a new beat.

  1. Load a loop or a long sound onto any pad and select it.
  2. Choose how to cut in the menu above the waveform.
  3. Press Hacka till pads (chop to pads).
ModeHow it cuts
Anslag (hits)Finds the hits in the sound and cuts at each one. The känslighet (sensitivity) bar sets how many hits it finds. Drag it right for more slices.

Chopping replaces the sounds on the pads it fills. You can also right-click a pad and choose Hacka detta ljud till alla pads (chop this sound to all pads), which uses the hits mode.

07The techno filter

Every one of the 8 tracks has its own filter, distortion and sends. The panel on the right shows the selected track, for example SPÅR 3. Make sure the FILTER tab is chosen. The EUKLID tab is explained in chapter 08.

Filter type

LP 24 and LP 12 are low-pass (dark), HP is high-pass (thin), BP is band-pass (nasal) and Notch cuts a narrow band. Av (off) bypasses the filter.

Polyrhythms in practice

Set the kick to 16 steps. Set a perc to 13 steps. The perc lands one step earlier against the kick each bar, and only lines up again after 13 bars. Now set a clap to 5-del: five steps per beat against the kick's four. Small changes in length and rate give grooves that keep shifting for minutes.

Euclidean mode

Press E on a row. Now you choose only how many hits and BETONG spreads them as evenly as possible over the track's length. Rotation shifts the whole pattern forward. Classic shapes: 3 hits in 8 steps, 5 in 8, 7 in 16. Editing a step by hand turns Euclidean mode off again.

Playing

BETONG follows your DAW: it starts and stops with the DAW and uses its tempo. Without a DAW, or in the standalone app, press SPELA. It changes to STOPP while running. While the DAW plays, the button says DAW and is locked.

Scenes and fill

A B C D above the tracks are four scenes: four complete patterns in the same kit. Click a scene while the beat plays and it blinks until the next bar, then switches exactly on the downbeat. When stopped it switches at once. Kopiera till... (copy to...) copies the scene you see to another one, so you can make a variation and keep the original.

Hold FILL for a drum roll: the clap, snare and tom tracks (pads 5–8) roll in sixteenths and get louder towards the end of the bar, and the other tracks double their hits. The kick keeps going. Let go and the pattern is back.

SVAJ, GLITCH and DENSITET

Three bars along the top of the sequencer change how the pattern plays, without changing the pattern itself. Turn them down and everything is clean again.

BarWhat it does
SVAJ(sway) Hits drag a little behind the beat, their strength varies and the pitch drifts a few cents, like an old machine that wanders. The kick sways only half as much, so the groove holds.
GLITCHRandom bursts, reversed hits, pitch jumps, cut-off hits and dropped hits. The kick glitches rarely.
DENSITET(density) Thins out or fills up the whole beat. Euclidean tracks add or remove hits and keep them evenly spread. Other tracks drop or add soft hits. Turn it up into a drop, down into a break.

Euclidean magic: the EUKLID tab

Select a track and choose the EUKLID tab in the panel on the right. Everything here is calculated while the beat plays. The pattern itself never changes.

ControlWhat it does
ROTERA SJÄLV(rotate by itself) The track moves one step every 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8 or 16 bars. A Euclidean pattern always stays Euclidean, so it keeps grooving while it slowly turns.
ANDAS(breathe) The number of hits sweeps up and down by the amount you set, over 4–32 bars. Build-ups and breaks happen by themselves. On normal tracks it makes the density breathe instead.
ACCENTLAGER(accent layer) Its own Euclidean pattern of strong hits, with its own length. Give it a length that differs from the track, for example 3 in 8 over a 16-step track, and the accents walk around.
RATCHETLAGER(ratchet layer) Its own pattern of bursts: how many, over how many steps, and 2–4 hits per burst.
TONLAGER(pitch layer) Its own pattern of pitch jumps, in semitones. On a bass track the line tangles itself up and untangles again, because the layer lands in a new place each time round.
FYLL LUCKORNA I(fill the gaps in) The track only plays where another track is silent. If the track has no steps of its own, it fills every gap. Gives tight, interlocking African patterns that never collide.
Nollställ magin(reset the magic) Turns all of the above off for the track.

The ring view

Press RING above the tracks. The tracks turn into rings inside each other, with track 1 on the outside. Each dot is a step: big dots are hits, ringed dots are strong, faint dots are weak. A white circle shows where each track is playing, so you can watch a 7-step ring turn against a 16-step ring.

  • Drag around a ring to rotate that track.
  • Drag straight up or down, or scroll, to add or remove hits. This turns on Euclidean mode.
  • Double-click a ring to turn Euclidean mode on or off.

Next to the rings each track shows its length, rate and any Euclidean magic it has. Press RING again to go back to the step grid. The pattern folder Euklid-magi has 14 examples to start from.

09The MOTOR: beats that live

Press MOTOR above the tracks and the beat starts to live on its own. Tracks come and go with the energy, the pattern changes a little every phrase, filters breathe and sounds get thrown into the delay. Your pattern never changes: the MOTOR calculates a living copy while it plays. The same seed always sounds the same, so DAW loops and exports sound exactly like what you heard.

The MOTOR tab

Open the MOTOR tab in the panel on the right. At the top: MOTOR PÅ (on), the form, LÄGE (ready-made motor settings), the seed and NY (a new seed).

FormWhat happens
Bara liv(just life) No song form. Everything plays, with small changes and breathing filters.
Fri(free) Endless waves of energy, with a break and a lift now and then.
Kort 64, Låt 128, Afterhour 256(short, song, afterhour) A full track over 64, 128 or 256 bars: intro, build, peak, break, lift, peak 2 and outro. Bar 1 in your DAW is the start of the intro.

The energy curve shows the whole form with the play position. Click it to switch to the evolution map: one row per track and one column per bar. Brighter means more hits, dark means the track rests, and a cold line on top means the MOTOR has changed the pattern in that bar.

KnobWhat it does
RÖRELSE(movement) How often things change: from every 32 bars to every 2 bars.
DJUP(depth) How big each change is. One hit is added, removed, moved, made stronger or weaker, or retuned. After four changes the original pattern comes back, and sometimes an old motif returns, slightly changed.
KAOS(chaos) Pulls everything at once: more movement and depth, a rougher energy curve, more drops and some glitch.
FILTERFilters breathe slowly, close before every change and open when the new part comes in. Resonance peaks when the energy is high, a darker master in the break and a rising high-pass in the lift.
DUBAt the end of a phrase one hit is thrown into the dub delay and fades out.
PUMPSidechain from the kick and drive that grow with the energy.
SVÄNG(swing) Velocity waves with their own lengths, micro-timing (hats drag, the kick stays on the grid) and swing that drifts slowly.

In the sequencer you see the MOTOR work live: a cold frame is an added hit, a crossed-out step is a removed one, a cold dot is a changed velocity or pitch, and a resting track turns dark. Tracks with Lås (lock) are left alone, and empty tracks stay empty.

Trig conditions

Right-click a step and choose Villkor (condition). 1:4 plays only the first time round out of four, 3:4 only the third, and so on up to 8:8. FILL plays only while FILL is held, !FILL only when it is not. PRE plays if the previous condition on the track was true, !PRE if it was not. The condition is shown at the top of the step.

Game of Life and call and response

Right-click the name of a track.

  • Game of Life: the steps become cells that are born and die once per bar. Puls (rule 90) ripples, Kaos (rule 30) is wild, Liv is a gentle one-dimensional Life. The pattern you drew is the first generation, and every 16 bars life starts over from it. New hits are soft ghost notes.
  • Fråga och svar (call and response): set one track to Fråga (call) and another to Svar (response). They take turns in blocks of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars.

Breed and morph

  • AVLA (breed) pairs the scene that plays with another scene. Each track inherits its base from one parent, the steps are crossed over at a random point and a small mutation is added. The child goes into a third scene and starts on the next bar. Ångra takes it back.
  • MORF slides the steps over to another scene, step by step. Right-click it to choose the scene. Automate it in your DAW for long transitions.

Drag out as MIDI

Drag the MIDI button on the MOTOR tab into your DAW. What plays, with the MOTOR, life, conditions, morph, swing and micro-timing, becomes a MIDI file with one track per sequencer track, starting at bar 1. Drum tracks use pad notes (C1 = pad 1). Bass, stabs and leads use real pitches with F2 as the root. Click the button to choose the length or to save the file.

AI help without keys

  1. Press AI and choose Kopiera AI-fråga (copy AI question). BETONG puts a ready-made question on the clipboard. It describes the format, the sounds on your pads and the pattern that plays.
  2. Paste it into any AI chat (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude) and write what you want at the top: a style, a feeling, a track or an artist.
  3. Copy the whole answer and choose Klistra in AI-svar (paste AI answer). The pattern, swing and MOTOR settings load. Locked tracks are left alone, and Ångra takes it back.

No account, no key and nothing is sent anywhere by BETONG. The AI only knows what has been written about a track, it cannot listen to it, so treat the answer as a starting point.

Patterns to start from

The pattern folder Motor och liv uses conditions, Game of Life and call and response. Load one, press MOTOR and choose a LÄGE, for example Hypnotisk minimal or Plastik-drift.

Performance

STUTTER loops a short piece of the output while you hold it down. Pick the length in the menu next to it: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32. TEMPO sets BETONG's own tempo (60–200 BPM) when it plays without a DAW.

11MIDI and automation

Play the pads from a keyboard

BETONG listens to MIDI notes 36–51, so 16 keys play pads 1–16. Note 36 is called C1 in most DAWs (C3 in FL Studio). Velocity sets how loud the pad plays. You can play pads by MIDI while the sequencer runs, for fills on top of the pattern.

NotesPads
36–43Pads 1–8 (top row)
44–51Pads 9–16 (bottom row)

Automation

Every knob and switch can be automated from your DAW: the effects, master, swing, SVAJ, GLITCH, DENSITET, FILL, all 8 track filters and track swing, and pitch, decay, volume, pan and reverse on all 16 pads. In the DAW they have Swedish names, for example Spår 3 cutoff (track 3 cutoff) or Pad 5 pitch.

12Recipes

Hypnotic loop in five minutes

  1. Load the kit Industriell 909 and turn GALENSKAP to about half.
  2. Lock the kick track with Lås. Roll MÖNSTER until you hear a groove you like.
  3. Set one perc track to 7 or 13 steps, and another to 5-del.
  4. Send that perc to the delay with SÄND DELAY, and set the delay to 3/16.
  5. Automate SVEP slowly over 32 bars.

Euclidean hypnosis

  1. Load the kit Betong Grund and the pattern Grund 130.
  2. Select the perc track, press E and set 5 hits in 13 steps.
  3. On the EUKLID tab set ROTERA SJÄLV to every 2nd bar and ANDAS to 3 over 16 bars.
  4. Select the bass track and give it a TONLAGER of 2 in 7 at +12.
  5. Open RING and watch it turn. Ride DENSITET into the drop.

Rebuild a loop

  1. Drag a drum loop onto pad 1.
  2. Choose Anslag and press Hacka till pads.
  3. Point the tracks at the slices and program a new order. Turn on Baklänges on one slice.

A whole track from one loop

  1. Load the pattern Livets Hats and the kit Plastik Minimal.
  2. Press MOTOR, choose the form Låt 128 and the mode Hypnotisk minimal.
  3. Press play in your DAW from bar 1 and let it run. Press NY for a new version of the same track.
  4. When you like it, drag the MIDI button into your DAW, or record the audio.

The break

  1. Turn up SÄND DELAY on the clap, then press Freeze on the delay.
  2. Turn SVEP slowly to the right to thin out the whole mix.
  3. Hold STUTTER at 1/16 for the last bar, then let go and turn SVEP back to the centre.

14SMIDET, the Euclidean forge

SMIDET is the second machine in the zip. It shares BETONG's engine but has its own window, sounds, kits and patterns. It has no pads and no waveform. The Euclidean rings are the whole instrument.

  • The rings fill the left half. Each lane is a ring. Drag around a ring to rotate it, drag up or down (or scroll) to change the number of hits, and double-click to switch Euclid on or off. Click a ring to select its lane.
  • M, S and + next to each lane in the ring list: mute, solo and load your own sample. Muted lanes turn dark in the ring.
  • Your own samples: drop audio files on a ring or a lane name. Several files land on that lane and the lanes below it. Ladda egen sampling... in the forge panel does the same for the selected lane.
  • Right-click a ring or lane name for mute, solo, lock, Euclid, library sounds, your own sample, preview, unmute all and clear all solo.
  • The forge panel on the right shows the selected lane: pad, rate, length, Euclid, hits, rotation, mute, solo and lock. The Ljud button picks a sound straight from the library, with the Smedja category first.
  • HAMMARE (hammer) makes everything denser and harder: more density, more glitch bursts, more drive and more clipper.
  • GLÖD (glow) heats the forge: more filter resonance, drive and envelope, more delay and more rumble.
  • Sounds: 16 forge sounds in the Smedja category, with anvils tuned to F minor, steel hammer, slag, sheet metal, chain, spring, quench, bellows, casting bell, pipes, scrap and sparks. There is also an anvil kick, forge hats and a hammer knock.
  • Kits: Smedjan, Järnverket, Klockgjuteriet, Valsverk Euklid, Rost och Gnistor and Kall Härdning.
  • Patterns: the Smide folder holds 14 fully Euclidean, polymetric patterns. SMIDET opens with Smedjans Puls and the Smedjeglöd effect preset.

Scenes, fill, SVAJ, GLITCH, DENSITET, the EUKLID tab, the filter and all effects work exactly as in BETONG.
